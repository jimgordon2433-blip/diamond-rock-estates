The Story

A Place Apart

There is a kind of quiet you only find above two thousand feet. It arrives at the edge of the hardwood forest, where the morning fog lifts off the ridgeline and the air turns cool against your skin. Here, the days are measured in birdsong and the slow turn of the light. This is the North Georgia you remember — or the one you have always meant to find.

Diamond Rock Estates is a small, gated community of custom mountain homes rising from forested homesites outside Ellijay, the Apple Capital of Georgia. Rock outcroppings, old oaks, long views — and homes built to belong here, raised by a family builder who has worked these mountains for two decades. Twenty-five minutes on, the restored streets of Blue Ridge wait with their scenic railway, their farm tables, their unhurried afternoons.

Choose your ridge. Build something beautiful. Light a fire as the stars come out, and let the mountain do the rest.

Build With Us

Homes as Rare as the Land

Diamond Rock Estates is a community of custom mountain homes — not simply lots on a map. Choose a homesite, bring your vision, and build a home worthy of the ridge it rests on. Or step into one of our completed residences, finished and ready for the first fire.

Every home is raised by Kasteel Enterprises, a family builder based in Ellijay with two decades of custom work across these mountains. The same hands shaping this community will shape your home — with the craft and quiet confidence a legacy property deserves.

Ellijay & Blue Ridge

The Town at Your Doorstep

Ellijay

Apple orchards in the fall and trailheads year round. The Cohutta Wilderness and Appalachian Trail begin nearby, vineyards line the back roads, and the festival calendar fills every season with something worth the drive.

Blue Ridge

Twenty-five minutes north: the Scenic Railway, tubing on the Toccoa, antique shops and quiet boutique dining, and a lake made for slow mornings. The Chattahoochee National Forest wraps it all.
