Who We Are

One family. One mountain.

No fund. No conglomerate. Just land that has been held, walked, and loved by the same family — now opened, carefully, to a few good neighbors.

Our Story

Held, Not Flipped

Most mountain land in North Georgia passes through corporate hands before it reaches yours — assembled by a development fund, carved into the maximum number of lots, and sold by people who have never spent a night on the ridge. Diamond Rock Estates is not that.

The entire mountain is family owned. It has been part of our family for generations, and we still walk these trails, know where the springs run, and watch the same fog settle into the same hollows every morning. When we decided to share it, we made a quiet promise: keep the lots few, keep the trees standing, and choose neighbors the way you would choose friends.

That is why there is no sales office and no high-pressure pitch. There is a family, a small number of homesites, and a pair of local people we trust to help you find your place here. We are not in a hurry. The mountain has waited a long time. It can wait for the right people.

“We are not selling a development. We are letting a few people into a place we love.”
